# Task 2: Analyze a Phishing Email Sample

## Objective

Identify phishing characteristics in a suspicious email sample.

## Files Included

* phishing_email_sample.txt
* Phishing_Email_Analysis.docx
* Sample Email Screenshot

## Key Findings

* Suspicious sender email address
* Generic greeting
* Urgent and threatening language
* Suspicious URL
* Possible email spoofing
* Social engineering tactics

## Summary

A phishing email sample was analyzed to identify common phishing indicators such as suspicious sender address, urgent language, fake links, social engineering techniques, and account suspension threats.

## Outcome

Successfully identified phishing characteristics and gained awareness of phishing attacks, email spoofing, and threat detection techniques.
